Chas Hack

The First Student Hackathon

The premiere hackathon at Chas Academy, organized by students for students. A day of creativity, collaboration, and innovation with the theme "Simplify" — turning chaos into clarity.

The Vision

Chas Hack was born from a simple idea: students inspiring students. As first-year students at Chas Academy, we knew firsthand how powerful hackathons can be for learning, networking, and pushing creative boundaries. We wanted to create a welcoming space where everyone could participate — no prior experience required.

Theme: Simplify

From chaos to clarity. The challenge was to take something complicated, boring, or messy in school or everyday life and build a solution that makes it simple and efficient. Teams had freedom to choose any tech stack — from console apps to full web applications — with AI tools encouraged.

"The expectation wasn't to build an enterprise-grade product. The goal was to get as far as possible, present an MVP demo, and explain how the project could evolve. Creativity and innovation over perfection."
